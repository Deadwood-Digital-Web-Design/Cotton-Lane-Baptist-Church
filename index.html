Welcome

Whoever you are, there's a seat for you.

Cotton Lane Baptist is a church family that gathers to worship, open the Bible together, and look out for one another.

Maybe you've been in church your whole life. Maybe it's been years. Either way, you're welcome here. No dress code, no pressure. Come on a Sunday or a weeknight, say hello, and see if it feels like home.
