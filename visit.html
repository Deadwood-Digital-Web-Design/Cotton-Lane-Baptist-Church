What to expect

  1. Park right out front

    There's plenty of room in the lot, with accessible parking close to the entrance.

  2. Come as you are

    Wear whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best.

  3. Say hello

    Introduce yourself to anyone. We're a church family and we love meeting new people.

  4. No pressure

    Sit where you like, take it all in, and stay as long as you want. Visiting costs nothing.
